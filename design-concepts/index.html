Unbound · first-person awakening meets neuroscience

Five ways to
hold the same territory.

Five working prototypes, each built from the real copy in content/ and data/site-data.json: all eight experiences, Angelo's and Dōgen's quotes with translators, the brain-region links, the five-stage map and the honest limits. Every concept keeps the site's core move: the inside and the behind-the-scenes of one experience, side by side.

01

Ensō

Sumi ink on washi paper

A brushed ensō draws itself on load, and the eight experiences sit around the circle in the order of the site's navigation graph. The circle never quite closes, a nod to "the loop closes" at The click. Opening one unrolls a hanging scroll with three voices: 今 the modern teacher, 古 the ancestor, 脳 behind the scenes.

Mood
Quiet, reverent, Zen. Dōgen leads.
Signature
Animated brushstroke, vermilion seals, a stage map drawn as ensō that open wider at each stage.
Trade-off
The most traditional. It could read as "a Buddhism site" more than a science one.

02

Two Sides

Candlelight on the left, lab instruments on the right

The bottom line, "pointing at the same territory from opposite sides", built as the interface. A draggable seam splits the screen into a warm first-person half and a cold oscilloscope half. Each experience gets its own animated instrument: beta suppressing in the TPJ, intrinsic and extrinsic networks falling into phase, Lempel-Ziv complexity rising, and a flat dashed line for The click, which no lab has captured. Dōgen scrolls past underneath like a ticker from 800 years ago.

Mood
Cinematic, a little dramatic, very "science".
Signature
Drag the seam to favour either side. A ⚠ Honest limits drawer.
Trade-off
The scopes are illustrative schematics, labelled as such. Making them data-driven would need real figures.

03

Constellation

A night sky you can navigate

The eight experiences are stars, and the connections from the content doc ("space ↔ time co-dissolve", "ground → recognition") are the constellation lines, which light up with their labels on hover. Choosing a star zooms the sky toward it and a card rises that literally flips: one face is the experience, the other is the neuroscience. Angelo's stages become moon phases, with Contraction as a dim waning moon.

Mood
Wonder, exploration, vastness.
Signature
Zoom-to-star, 3D card flip (the original "flip each card" brief, taken literally).
Trade-off
The most "delightful" at first touch, but stars and space are a familiar metaphor.

04

Field Atlas

A risograph print magazine

A bold editorial take: misregistered pink and blue riso type, a generative motif per entry (a clock with no hands for The eternal now, lock tumblers for The click), and each experience as a two-page spread. The right page carries a schematic brain figure with the linked regions pulsing. Red-pencil marginalia ("n = 1", "authors flag demand characteristics!") sit next to the findings they qualify, so the honest limits live inline instead of in a footnote. An "Errata" appendix closes it.

Mood
Smart, confident, editorial, playful.
Signature
Brain figures, red-pencil caveats, Dōgen in a "from the archive" box.
Trade-off
Long scroll. The brain figure is schematic, not anatomical.

05

Stop.

A site that practises what it describes

It opens gently on “Just this.” and a hold-to-enter button. Faint narrator thoughts (“am I doing this right”, “tomorrow”) drift across the screen. A full-width Me-ing ↔ Being river runs along the bottom, choppy and dark on one side and calm and gold on the other. Dragging along it dissolves the thoughts and the interface while the colours grow more vivid. Each experience starts with a guided minute that resets the river to the middle and lets it drift toward Being. Only then do the quote, the ancestor, the brain and a “But is this real?” research card open up.

Mood
Calm, experiential, intimate.
Signature
The UI performs the experience: the narrator fades and the world gets vivid.
Trade-off
The most opinionated. It puts practice before information, which won't suit a reader who wants the science fast.

Notes

  • All five read from one shared file, data.js, generated from data/site-data.json, plus the navigation edges, limits and field notes from the markdown docs. Nothing is invented copy except the narrator thoughts in Stop.
  • The content's guardrails are kept everywhere: Angelo isn't framed as a neuroscience teacher, every Dōgen quote carries its translator, and "correlation, not causation" appears next to the science.
  • The pieces mix well: the Two Sides oscilloscopes could sit on the back of the Constellation cards, and the Stop slider could live on any of them.
