# The science, straight — 7 themes in plain language

Written for the site's reference section. Each theme: a plain-language explainer, the key findings, and the studies. Caveats live in `limits.md`; the sharpest ones are noted inline where they matter most.

## 1. The predictive mind: perception as controlled hallucination

The brain doesn't passively receive the world — it *predicts* it, then corrects its predictions with sensory data. Your experience at any moment is the brain's best guess, weighted by how much it trusts its priors versus the incoming signal. Meditation, on this view, is **willful prior relaxation**: deliberately turning down the precision-weighting on high-level beliefs — including the highest-level belief of all, the narrative self.

- **REBUS** (Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019): psychedelics "relax the precision of high-level priors… thereby liberating bottom-up information flow." The DMN sits at the top of the hierarchy, so its relaxation is felt as ego dissolution. The paper's own meditation section: "the relaxation of prior assumptions (high-level priors), whether done willfully or with the assistance of a psychedelic, heightens an individual's openness and receptivity" — meditation as *willful* prior relaxation.
- **The continuum** (Laukkonen & Slagter, 2021): focused attention → open monitoring → non-dual meditation progressively relinquish "increasingly engrained habits of prediction," reducing the "temporal depth" of the predictive hierarchy "until all conceptual processing falls away, unveiling a state of pure awareness." Insight = Bayesian model reduction (pruning redundant parameters).
- **Empirical test** (Fucci et al., 2018): expert meditators in Open Presence (non-dual) vs. focused attention during an auditory oddball task — Open Presence showed "increased sensory monitoring and reduced perceptual inferences," i.e. relaxed prior-formation, with increased gamma (25–40 Hz). [Note: a possible 2021 Bayesian replication suggested null effects — verify before citing.]
- **The open gate** (Mylius et al., 2025): expert meditators didn't detect faint touches better — they had a *lower decision criterion* (more liberal responding), preceded by lower prestimulus alpha: "keeping the gate more open," relying less on priors, more on data.
- **Less sticky priors** (Yang et al., 2025): 7T fMRI of an adept through insight stages — advanced meditation "reduces the 'stickiness' of prior predictions… and attractor and feedback loops," letting processing happen at earlier, rawer levels.

## 2. Networks: the self quiets, the wall dissolves, integration remains

- **The narrator quiets.** The DMN (PCC, mPFC) runs self-referential thought; it deactivates in experienced meditators, PCC quieting in the deepest states (Brewer et al., 2011). Real-time fMRI neurofeedback linked PCC deactivation to "effortless awareness" — with the authors' own caveat about demand characteristics (Garrison et al., 2013).
- **The wall dissolves.** The intrinsic (self-related) and extrinsic (task/world) networks are normally anticorrelated; during non-dual-awareness meditation specifically, the anticorrelation breaks down — both stay active at once (Josipovic et al., 2012).
- **The integrator.** Josipovic (2024) proposes the **central precuneus network** (central precuneus + dlPFC, dACC, dmPFC, IPL) as the neural correlate of non-dual awareness: uniquely able to couple with *both* intrinsic and extrinsic systems. The **salience network** (anterior insula, dACC) does the reverse — monitoring that *increases* segregation. Subcortical arousal systems are "necessary, not sufficient."
- **Reorganization, not just deactivation.** Reviewing proficient meditators: decreased posterior DMN, increased central executive network activity, increased long-range theta/alpha/gamma coherence, expanded neural dynamics — "dynamic reorganization toward global synchronization" underlying "self-environment unity, i.e., nondual awareness" (Cooper, Ventura & Northoff).
- **Posterior integration wins (2025).** The preregistered **COGITATE** adversarial collaboration (N=256; fMRI, MEG, iEEG) testing integrated information theory vs. global workspace theory: posterior cortex decoded conscious contents at >95% accuracy across the full stimulus duration; prefrontal "ignition" reached ~70% and was temporally restricted. Sustained posterior integration — the temporo-parieto-occipital "hot zone" — is the current best-supported basis of "what it is like" (COGITATE Consortium, 2025, Nature). Josipovic bridges it: non-dual awareness as "the broadcast of the system's current ground state… the broadcast of its own broadcasting capacity."

## 3. Electrical signatures: what non-dual states look like in EEG/MEG

- **Beta tracks the boundary.** An expert meditator (20,000+ hrs) producing graded self-boundary dissolution: right-lateralized **beta oscillations in TPJ + medial parietal cortex** tracked dissolution depth; at maximum dissolution the phenomenological category of *time* vanished (Dor-Ziderman et al., 2016). Preregistered replication (N=46): **high-beta (21–30 Hz) suppression** in frontoparietal/posterior-medial cortices in "full dissolvers" — correlating with lifetime hours and interview-derived change, but *not* with classical self-report scales (Trautwein et al., 2024).
- **De-differentiation.** Boundary dissolution raised broadband **Lempel-Ziv complexity** (with local orbitofrontal reductions) — the brain becoming less differentiated, mirroring the psychedelic pattern (Atad et al., 2025).
- **Pure awareness decoded.** 33 TM practitioners vs. controls: "pure awareness" was marked by **temporal entropy and aperiodic dynamics** (moment-to-moment unpredictability + slow coordinated distant activity); classic alpha phase-coherence contributed *least*; ML separated meditation from counting at 88% within-subject; and it's a **state, not a trait** (Canales-Johnson et al., 2026).
- **Depth decoded.** Time-varying meditative depth (1–5) decoded from EEG theta/alpha/gamma connectivity in 34 experts — while pre-selected DMN regions of interest "fell short" (Reggente et al., 2025).
- **Cessation, the limiting case.** In an intensive single-case study of meditative cessation (complete voluntary discontinuation of awareness), **alpha power collapsed ~40 s before onset**; the companion review frames advanced meditation as *disintegration* of the normally unified conscious space — the inverse of non-dual integration — and distinguishes cessation from pure consciousness and non-dual awareness (Chowdhury et al., 2023; Laukkonen et al., 2023).
- **Gamma rises with depth.** In Sri Lankan forest-monastery adepts moving through four deepening "Essence-of-Mind" states, **gamma current density increased in ACC, precuneus, and superior parietal lobule** — deepening non-duality recruits integration hubs with *more* high-frequency activity, not global quieting (Brown et al., 2018).

## 4. Time: how the brain builds "now," and what happens when it stops

- **The insula translates body into time.** Posterior insula activation climbs with stimulus duration during reproduction tasks; right anterior insula (arousal) dilates felt time, left contracts it (Craig's model; Pollatos et al., 2014).
- **Division of labor** (2026 review): cerebellum stabilizes sub-second timing; basal ganglia accumulate evidence over longer durations via dopamine; cortex integrates temporal priors — with the first formal model linking these loops to felt duration via a gain factor (Hajnal et al., 2026).
- **Temporal thickness collapses.** In active inference, ego dissolution = collapse of the "temporal thickness" of the self-model — the capacity to model consequences over time — via lowered precision on high-level priors (Deane, 2020).
- **Self and time co-dissolve.** 22 highly experienced meditators: meditation made body boundaries less salient, reduced attention to time, sped felt time passage — while metronome timing was unchanged: the effect is on higher-order time *experience*, not the inner clock (Linares Gutiérrez et al., 2022).
- **"Nowness" is itself temporal.** Metzinger: "temporal self-location" = being present in an experienced "now"; "'Nowness' itself is a form of temporal experience" — so minimal phenomenal experience suspends time representation itself (Metzinger, 2020). His survey (N=1,403) found 12 factors in pure-consciousness reports, including "Time, Effort and Desire" and "Emptiness and Non-egoic Self-awareness" (Gamma & Metzinger, 2021). Philosophical gloss: meditative "timelessness" = indeterminate duration lacking temporal structure (change, order, succession), with duration itself remaining (Frischhut, 2024).

## 5. Space and the bodily self: the TPJ story

- **The orientation area.** SPECT of meditators in deep states: decreased superior parietal activity (the area mapping the body's position in space), inversely related to frontal attention increases — accompanying "the sense of no space and no time" (Newberg et al., 2001; same pattern in contemplative nuns).
- **TPJ encodes self-location — causally.** Focal stimulation of the right angular gyrus *elicited* out-of-body experiences (Blanke et al., 2002, Nature). Visual-tactile conflict in VR relocated the felt self outside the body (Lenggenhager et al., 2007, Science). Lesions of TPJ produce pathological self-location errors (Ionta et al., 2011, Neuron). Visual-vestibular congruency dials OBE-like sensations up and down (Wu et al., 2023).
- **The takeaway for the site:** self-location is a constructed, manipulable integration product — which is why a practice can dissolve it.

## 6. The psychedelic overlap: where the analogy holds — and breaks

Hold: both routes relax high-level priors; both show DMN disruption and ego-dissolution phenomenology; narratives converge (near-identical mystical-experience scores in an NLP comparison of psychedelic vs. meditation reports; Kallio-Mannila et al., 2025). Under LSD, ego-dissolution ratings tracked DMN disintegration — specifically *de-integrated coupling* (e.g. retrosplenial–parahippocampal decoupling), not shutdown (Carhart-Harris et al., 2016; Tagliazucchi et al., 2016; via Millière et al., 2018).

Break (Millière et al., 2018): (i) psychedelic self-loss is usually **content-flooded** (vivid hallucinations) vs. meditation's sparse contentlessness — any model needs a "phenomenological bandwidth" dimension (one exception: 5-MeO-DMT's contentless "nothing," resembling samadhi); (ii) meditation involves **attentional control and agency** that drug states lack; (iii) it's an open question whether "non-dual awareness" and "pure consciousness" are even **distinct, valid constructs** — "not yet enough data." Also: **two routes to narrative self-loss** — (a) cessation of self-referential thought/mental time travel (both routes), vs. (b) loss of access to semantic autobiographical information, more psychedelic-specific. And the narratives diverge emotionally: psychedelic reports carried higher positive *and* negative sentiment — REBUS predicts relaxed constraints allow more varied intense emotion, while meditation's gradual approach yields a more balanced state (Kallio-Mannila et al., 2025).

Framing for the site: psychedelics are the *forced* version of what meditation does *willfully* — same predictive machinery, different reliability, agency, and phenomenal bandwidth.

## 7. The frontier: 2023–2026

The field is moving fast. Anchors for the site: Josipovic's central precuneus proposal (2024); the COGITATE adversarial result for posterior integration (2025, Nature); Trautwein's preregistered MEG replication of beta-suppression in boundary dissolution (2024); Canales-Johnson's pure-awareness EEG signature (2026); Yang's 7T insight-stage mapping (2025); the formal κ-gain model of subjective time (Hajnal et al., 2026); Metzinger's open-access synthesis of 500+ pure-consciousness reports from 57 countries (2024, *The Elephant and the Blind*). Unverified leads the site should *not* cite as findings yet: Daws et al. 2022 on subacute psilocybin and global integration; a possible null replication of the Fucci MMN effect; Laukkonen/Friston/Chandaria's active-inference model of meditative timelessness ("A beautiful loop").
