# Angelo DiLullo — bio, map, vocabulary, key quotes

## Who he is (for the site's "teacher" panel)

**Angelo DiLullo, MD** — board-certified in Family Medicine and Anesthesiology, based near Denver, working full-time as a physician. Founder of **Simply Always Awake** (simplyalwaysawake.com; ~986 podcast episodes as of early 2026). Author of ***Awake: It's Your Turn*** (2021; ~4.6 stars, 500+ Goodreads ratings). Began teaching publicly about 15 years after his own awakening, starting with casual conversations with friends.

**His story in brief.** As a child he recalls "being in presence most of the time and noticing thoughts" — then thoughts "started crowding in and defining me," until by his early teens "something was pervasively wrong" and he concluded "it was wrong with me." At 18–19 he learned Transcendental Meditation ("like water in the desert," but only a respite). At 24, after a breakup, he read Kapleau's *The Three Pillars of Zen*, read the Kensho accounts and thought: *"I don't know how this is going to happen for me, but this is going to happen for me, if it kills me."* He devised his own self-inquiry — becoming fascinated by what a thought even was, "literally waiting for the next thought, orienting and watching it arise" — and awakened across two consecutive nights: first, *"literally everything was pure conscious being… There was no personal identity, there was a universal consciousness"*; the next day, *"the whole paradigm of identity… just completely disappeared, and it never came back."*

**Important correction for the site:** Angelo did **not** write *The Power of Awe* (that's Jake Eagle & Michael Amster). His only book is *Awake: It's Your Turn*.

**His relationship to science:** He does not teach from neuroscience — he teaches from direct experience and inquiry. But he is science-literate and science-compatible (physician background): he references the default mode network casually, cites quantum field theory ("there is no matter… It's one thing"), and uses the photon-timelessness pointer while explicitly flagging "I'm talking experientially now. I'm not making a statement about science." **The site must not present him as a "neuroscience of awakening" teacher — he isn't one.** The marriage of his first-person accounts with neuroscience is the site's own editorial project.

## His 5-stage map (interactive timeline)

Angelo warns that both over- and under-emphasizing stages are ways of "distancing ourselves from life" — present the map as a rough sketch, not a ladder.

1. **Awakening** — "the first shift in identity" (stream-entry / Kensho). Subject-object in consciousness collapses; "I am everything" / pure "amness."
2. **Honeymoon** — "you feel like there are no boundaries anywhere, non-doership is obvious, it's wonderful." Lasts weeks to months, sometimes years.
3. **Contraction / shadow work** — expansion ("This is how unfiltered reality actually is") gives way to contraction ("And this is what's preventing you from experiencing unfiltered reality on an ongoing basis"). "Flip-flopping of Me-ing and Being, like a roller coaster." Repressed emotion surfaces and is felt more directly than before — "a different kind of worse." Normal: "Suddenly experiencing a ton of shame, regrets, and fears — this is actually totally normal. Repressed material comes to the surface."
4. **Non-dual perception** — the subject-object construct dissolves *in the sense world itself*: "you start to truly experience non-duality — not experiencing the division between you and everything else." Not just "I am consciousness" but sense phenomena as non-dual.
5. **Liberation** — "when the sense of self in all its forms — all the selfing mechanisms — dissolve or drop away. That's the end of personal suffering because it's the end of personal anything." / "After liberation, identity is essentially dissolved into presence."

**Post-awakening guidance (for the map's "field notes"):** "Don't try to recreate your awakening. It will never happen. The past is gone." / "Be prepared to feel rather unenlightened for some time. This is shadow work; it cannot be avoided." / "Don't ever compare your own experience to anything I've said or anyone said. Your immediate experience is the best thing there to wake you up — just as it is." / On the body: "The body stores the sense of saying no to life. Go into the contracted area, feel that nervous restlessness… When you realize you can't control reality and you actually feel that, what most people notice later is the reaction is simply gone." / On teachers: "Look for a teacher that doesn't want anything from you at all."

## Glossary (his key terms)

- **Unbound consciousness** — his signature term: consciousness no longer bound by subject-object division. "When subjectification… stops happening, then you could call that unbound consciousness."
- **Subjectification** — "the movement in consciousness to create a subject and an object… so fundamental to our functioning… it's impossible to imagine what it's like without that operating."
- **Pristine, undivided nature** — his central noun phrase for what we are (from the book's subtitle/description).
- **Perceptual distortion / perceptual filters** — his framing device: awakening = "recognizing and reversing the ways in which we habitually reject life at various levels through perceptual distortion."
- **Me-ing and Being** — verb-form for the post-awakening flip-flop between selfing activity and pure being.
- **Transmission** — three kinds: (1) relational — hearing someone's description can "induce and encourage similar processes in you"; (2) direct pointing — "using language very purposefully to induce experiential insight"; (3) most important — "you can learn various strategies and inquiries to investigate directly for yourself."
- **Honeymoon / Contraction** — phases of the post-awakening arc (see stages).
- **Beliefs as nested layers** — "one belief is actually based on a deeper more fundamental hidden belief… that acts as your operating system." Deepest: "I am a separate self in the world of objects." Later work addresses beliefs "about the perceptual distortions of time and space."
